Flint, Michigan Facts:
- Flint was founded as a trading post along the Flint River in 1819 by fur trader Jacob Smith.
- Known historically as the "Vehicle City," it served as the birthplace of General Motors (GM), which was founded there in 1908.
- The city was the historic site of the 1936-1937 General Motors sit-down strike, a pivotal event that successfully established the United Automobile Workers (UAW) union.

Source: City of Flint Official Portal & Encyclopædia Britannica (https://www.cityofflint.com/about-flint/)
