Grand Blanc, Michigan Facts:
- The city's name literally translates to "Great White" in French, given by early French traders before 1800.
- It was originally an Indian campground settled by the Chippewa people before pioneer families began arriving in the 1820s.
- Grand Blanc was the very first community in the state of Michigan to consolidate its public school districts in 1904, initially utilizing horse-drawn school buses.

Source: City of Grand Blanc Official Website & Thumbwind History Archives (https://www.cityofgrandblancmi.gov/about_us/index.php)
